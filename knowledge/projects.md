## Detailed Project Case Studies

## 1. TruckFlow — Logistics Management Platform

- **Live URL:** [https://www.truckflowhq.com/](https://www.truckflowhq.com/)
- **Category:** SaaS / Logistics / Fleet Management
- **Description:** A mobile-first logistics management platform built for dispatch managers and truck drivers to monitor routes, coordinate dispatches, and track fleet activities in real time.
- **Technologies Used:** Next.js, React, Node.js, WebSockets (Socket.io), Tailwind CSS, PWA, Authentication & Notification Providers.
- **Key Features:**
  - Real-time fleet tracking and dynamic driver route updates via WebSockets.
  - Multi-language translation support.
  - Push notification system and foreground alerts.
  - High performance, responsive mobile-optimized UI.

## 2. Start2Write — AI Writing Tutor Platform

- **Live URL:** [https://www.start2write.com/](https://www.start2write.com/)
- **Category:** AI / EdTech / Writing Assistant
- **Description:** An intelligent AI-powered writing tutor that teaches writers what changed, why it changed, and how to improve. Unlike basic grammar checkers, it focuses on pedagogical explanations and lasting skill development.
- **Technologies Used:** Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Google Gemini / AI APIs, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI.
- **Key Features:**
  - Interactive "Your Draft" real-time AI analyzer with instant explanations.
  - Tone switcher (formal, friendly, academic, concise).
  - Multi-step client feedback and educator collaboration portals.
  - Beautiful glassmorphic UI with smooth animations and responsive design.
